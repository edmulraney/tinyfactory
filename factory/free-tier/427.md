### #427. Both reviews in one job

- **Set:** `ci.yml` has one `review` job where it had `adam` and `ryan`. In it, `review.sh` runs both reviewers' sessions at the same time, started with the `claude` CLI in a checkout of the base, and then posts each verdict and saves each transcript (ticket #427; `decisions.md` [A427a](../decisions/427.md), [A427b](../decisions/427.md), [A427e](../decisions/427.md), [A427f](../decisions/427.md)). As first built, a second job, `verdicts`, posted and saved them, and the `review` job's token only read; [entry #541](541.md) has what that job cost and why it went.
- **Protects:** Actions minutes.
- **Why:** each job is billed rounded up to a whole minute, and each review job had a checkout and an install of its own.
- **Numbers, before:** from 14 to 18 November 2026 `ryan` was billed 900 minutes and `adam` 789, which is 1,689 together, in 378 jobs each ([entry #388](388.md)).
- **Numbers, after:** worked out from those jobs, not measured: a workflow cannot run before it merges. Each pair billed as its longer job alone is 954 minutes, 735 fewer than 1,689. `factory/scripts/minutes.sh <from> <to>` over a later week shows what `review` is billed.
- **What starting in the base's checkout costs:** no minute: it is one checkout, as before, and one `git worktree` for the pull request's files.
- **What it costs besides:** a `review` job run again after one review was cut short starts with both reviewers; the pass that stands is carried with no session (`decisions.md` [A160a](../decisions/160.md)), so only the other reviews again.
- **Automated:** yes.

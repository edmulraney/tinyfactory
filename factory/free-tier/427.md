### #427. Both reviews in one job

- **Set:** `ci.yml` has a `review` job and a `verdicts` job where it had `adam` and `ryan`. In `review`, `review.sh` runs both reviewers' sessions at the same time, started with the `claude` CLI, in a checkout of the base and with a token that only reads. `verdicts` then posts each verdict and saves each transcript, and runs none of the pull request's files (ticket #427; `decisions.md` [A427a](../decisions/427.md), [A427b](../decisions/427.md), [A427e](../decisions/427.md), [A427f](../decisions/427.md)).
- **Protects:** Actions minutes.
- **Why:** each job is billed rounded up to a whole minute, and each review job had a checkout and an install of its own.
- **Numbers, before:** from 14 to 18 November 2026 `ryan` was billed 900 minutes and `adam` 789, which is 1,689 together, in 378 jobs each ([entry #388](388.md)).
- **Numbers, after:** worked out from those jobs, not measured: a workflow cannot run before it merges. Each pair billed as its longer job alone is 954 minutes, 735 fewer than 1,689. The `verdicts` job gives back part of that: 378 more jobs. At 1 billed minute each, as `start-fix` is billed (120 minutes in 118 jobs), that is 378 minutes; at the 1.2 of `merge`, which like `verdicts` checks out the whole history (302 minutes in 249 jobs), it is 458. So the two jobs are billed between 1,332 and 1,412 minutes where `adam` and `ryan` were billed 1,689: 277 to 357 fewer, where one job for everything would have been 735 fewer. `factory/scripts/minutes.sh <from> <to>` over a later week shows what `review` and `verdicts` are billed.
- **What the two safety settings cost:** the session starting in the base's checkout costs no minute: it is one checkout, as before, and one `git worktree` for the pull request's files. The token that only reads costs the `verdicts` job, 378 to 458 of the 735 minutes.
- **Why `verdicts` is a job on every run:** it already starts on the event it answers, the end of the `review` job. It cannot be a step of that job, whose token must not write, and no other job runs after every review: `merge` runs only when everything passed and `start-fix` only when something failed.
- **What it costs besides:** a `review` job run again after one review was cut short starts with both reviewers; the pass that stands is carried with no session (`decisions.md` [A160a](../decisions/160.md)), so only the other reviews again.
- **Automated:** yes.

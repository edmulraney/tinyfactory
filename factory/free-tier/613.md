### #613. The delivery numbers are charted once a week

- **Set:** a second job of `review-notes.yml`, `metrics`, runs `metrics.sh` on Friday at 06:17 UTC and writes `metrics.md` on the `sessions` branch, with a time limit of 15 minutes (ticket #613; `decisions.md` [H613a](../decisions/613.md) to [H613g](../decisions/613.md)).
- **Protects:** Actions minutes.
- **Why:** a week's numbers are final only once the week has ended, and nothing happens then but time passing, so a schedule writes them, on the one the review notes already have.
- **Numbers, as set:** the parts measured on a laptop on 22 November 2026, the job not: a workflow cannot run before it merges. Listing 309 merged pull requests took 7 seconds, the rework of 41 pull requests of product tickets 146 seconds, a clone of the `sessions` branch (637 MB) 26 seconds and the turns of 210 pull requests 9 seconds: about 3 minutes, so about 4 billed minutes a week and 17 of the month's 2,000. The rework is worked out again for every product pull request each week ([H573a](../decisions/573.md)), about 3.5 seconds each, so the job grows by about a minute for every 17 that merge.
- **Numbers, the alternative:** the event-driven way writes the page after each merge. Week 47 of 2026 had 289 merges: at 4 minutes each, 1,156 minutes in one week, for a page read once a week.
- **Automated:** yes.

### #388. Where the Actions minutes of November 2026 went

- **Set:** nothing. `factory/scripts/minutes.sh <from> <to>` prints the billed minutes of a period by workflow, by job and by ticket, and this entry holds what it printed for 1 to 18 November 2026 and the changes that would cut it (ticket #388; `decisions.md` [H388a](../decisions/388.md) to [H388d](../decisions/388.md)).
- **Protects:** Actions minutes.
- **What happened:** by 18 November 2026 the owner's account had used the month's 2,000 free minutes and $30 more, and nobody knew on what.
- **Numbers, the whole:** 9,064 minutes, in the 1,169 runs that started a job, of about 2,800 runs created. Every run was created from 14 to 18 November. Printed one day at a time, the first four days are 186, 204, 2,470 and 4,051 minutes, which leaves 2,153 for 18 November up to the hour the period was printed. The printing's "Minutes it ran" column adds up to 7,413, so 1,651 are each job's last minute rounded up. The script adds up job times (`decisions.md` [H221b](../decisions/221.md)); what GitHub billed could not be read, because its usage report refuses a project's token.
- **Numbers, by ticket:** 171 tickets, 51 minutes each on average, half of them over 41, the largest 188 (#272). The limits table's 9 minutes is a ticket whose build and one `ci` run both pass first time. 323 minutes name no ticket.
- **Numbers, by workflow:** `ci` 4,464 in 330 runs, `implement` 2,408, `fix` 1,423, `retro` 473, `approve-merge` 193, `install-workflows` 57, every other 46.
- **Numbers, by job, billed and (run):**
  - `implement` 2,408 (2,256) in 260 jobs. 177 are builds, 13 minutes each; 81 ended inside a minute, a ticket refused a place (`decisions.md` [T64f](../decisions/64.md)).
  - `fix` 1,423 (1,323) in 171 jobs, and the old `fix` job of `ci` 115 in 25: 169 rounds ran an engineer, 9 minutes each.
  - `checks` 1,334 (1,153) in 401 jobs, 3.3 each, and `fits-main` 460 (386) in 153.
  - `ryan` 900 (704) and `adam` 789 (588), 378 jobs each.
  - `retro` 473 (399) in 149 jobs, 3.2 each.
  - `e2e` 444 (220) in 306 jobs, of which 169 ended inside a minute.
  - `merge` 302 (135) in 249 jobs, `ship` 180 (100) in 146, `start-fix` 120 (20) in 118, `install` 57 (14) in 57.
- **Numbers, fix rounds:** 84 of the 171 pull requests had a round, 164 rounds between them: 45 had one, 18 two, 21 three or more. A reviewer failed in 100 of the 141 failed `ci` runs, `checks` in 19, `fits-main` in 17. A `ci` run is billed 11 minutes, and a pull request had 2.3 of them. So a round is about 20 minutes: 8 for the engineer's job, 1 for `start-fix` and 11 for the run it repeats, and the 169 rounds took about 3,400 of the 9,064.
- **The plan, by minutes saved in the month, which is these five days:**
  1. **Agent jobs on a runner the owner names (#426): up to 6,108.** `implement`, `fix`, `retro`, `adam` and `ryan` are 67% of the minutes, and their runner mostly waits for Claude's API. GitHub charges nothing for a self-hosted runner's minute ([entry #426](426.md)).
  2. **The engineer reviews a build before handing it over (#428): about 850.** Worked out, not measured: if it catches half the 120 rounds a review started, 60 rounds at 20 minutes are 1,200, less 2 minutes more on each of 177 builds.
  3. **Both reviews in one job (#427): 735.** Measured: each pair of review jobs billed as its longer one alone is 954 minutes, against 1,689.
  4. **Only the factory tests a change can affect (#429): about 560.** The factory's tests are 145 of the 230 seconds of `checks` ([entry #268](268.md)); two minutes less in the 279 `checks` and `fits-main` jobs longer than 170 seconds is 558 minutes, and 90 seconds less is 409.
  5. **No retro after a single fix round (#430): about 160.** 50 retros ran for the 45 pull requests that merged after one round, at 3.2 minutes each.
- **Weighed, and no ticket:**
  - A public repository, where GitHub's runners are free: every minute, but the repo is private (`decisions.md` [S3](../decisions/setup.md)).
  - `start-fix` back inside another job, 120 minutes for 20 run: [entry 14](entry-14.md) moved it out because a round inside `ci` got no token.
  - No `e2e` job for a change outside the app, 169 minutes: each other way to decide costs the same minute or makes the reviews wait (`decisions.md` [M75a](../decisions/75.md)).
  - `merge` and `ship` inside another job, 482 minutes for 235 run: each needs every other job's result, and a job that waits for one is billed while it waits.
  - A refused build that starts no job, 81 minutes: a job has to run to count the tickets in flight (`decisions.md` [T64d](../decisions/64.md)).
- **Automated:** nothing was set. `minutes.sh` prints the breakdown again for any period.

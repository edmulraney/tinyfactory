### #241. Review notes are built once a week, in one ticket

- **Set:** `post-verdict.sh` adds each verdict's notes to one ticket, and `review-notes.yml` starts that ticket's build on Friday at 06:17 UTC (ticket #241; `decisions.md` A241a to A241c).
- **Protects:** Actions minutes, and the Claude subscription's usage.
- **Why:** a note fails no review, so acting on one needs a build, and a build costs the same 9 minutes and about $1 whether it applies one note or twenty.
- **Numbers, the alternative:** worked out, not measured. A ticket for each note: pull requests #235 and #237 carried 2 notes each, so 4 builds for two pull requests, 36 minutes and about $4. At 2 notes a pull request the factory's ceiling of about 220 tickets a month would fall to about 73.
- **Numbers, as set:** 1 billed minute each Friday for the job that starts the build, whether or not a ticket holds notes, and 9 minutes and about $1 for the build in a week that has one: 10 minutes a week, about 43 of the month's 2,000. Keeping a verdict's notes adds no job: three or four calls to GitHub in the review job that already runs.
- **What waits:** a note is applied up to a week after it was written, and later if no place is free on Friday morning: the ticket waits with `ready`, keeps collecting, and is started when a place frees (entry #269).
- **Automated:** yes.

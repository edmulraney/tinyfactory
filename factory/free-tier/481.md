### #481. Five tickets are built or checked at once

- **Set:** `in-flight.sh full` lets a build start while fewer than five other tickets are being built or checked, where it was three; the limit of five pull requests that wait for the owner stays (ticket #481; `decisions.md` [T64a](../decisions/64.md), [T361a](../decisions/361.md)). This replaces the numbers of [entry 16](entry-16.md) and [entry #361](361.md).
- **Protects:** the factory's speed, at the price of Neon's preview slots, Claude usage and fix rounds for a conflict.
- **Why:** since [entry #455](455.md) the pull requests that wait to merge are tested together, so one more in flight no longer costs a test each. What it costs now is conflicts, which the measures table counts so that a week shows it (`decisions.md` [H481](../decisions/481.md)).
- **Numbers, conflicts:** at a limit of three, on 18 and 19 November 2026, the owner counted 24 fix rounds for a conflict over 57 merged pull requests, 0.42 each (ticket #481). `measures.sh --week 2026-W47`, run on 20 November 2026 with two days of the week still to come, printed 0.29: 30 recorded rounds over 103 merged pull requests whose measures are recorded. A later week is compared with that figure. Three tickets in flight make 3 pairs that can conflict, and five make 10. A fix round is about 20 minutes of Actions ([entry #388](388.md)).
- **Numbers, database branches:** each open pull request that Vercel builds held one of Neon's 9 preview slots until its git branch was deleted ([entry 8](entry-8.md)), and since ticket #672 until its end-to-end tests pass ([entry #672](672.md)); one that changes only the factory's folders is not built and holds none ([entry #165](165.md)). Before, three being checked and five waiting held 8 of the 9 at most. Now five being checked and five waiting can ask for 10, one more than Neon has. When all ten change the app, the tenth finds no slot: its preview fails with "Branch limit reached", and a push after a slot frees builds it ([entry 8](entry-8.md)). At nine, no slot is left for a pull request merged since checks last ran, a person's own pull request, or a branch a cut-short build left ([entry 13](entry-13.md)). On 16 November 2026 Vercel built 13 of the 37 merged pull requests ([entry #165](165.md)); at that share ten open pull requests hold about 4 slots.
  - Rejected, four pull requests waiting for the owner at most, so that nine is never asked for: ticket #481 keeps five.
- **Claude usage:** not a figure the factory can read. Five builds at once draw on one subscription's limits up to five times as fast as one; a session the limit cuts short keeps its work, and no agent starts until the limit resets ([entry #395](395.md)).
- **Automated:** yes.

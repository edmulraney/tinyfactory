### #249. The factory's test files run at the same time

- **Set:** `test-factory.sh` runs the files in `factory/tests/` as many at once as the machine has processors, where it ran them one after another (ticket #249; `decisions.md` H249a to H249c). No workflow changed.
- **Protects:** Actions minutes.
- **Why:** the suite of entry 9 grew from 8 files to 65, and `pnpm test` runs in both the `checks` and `fits-main` jobs of every `ci` run.
- **Numbers, before:** in 16 clean `ci` runs on 17 November 2026, `pnpm test` took 2.8 minutes of the `checks` job's 3.7 and 2.7 of `fits-main`'s 3.5: 5.5 of a run's 9.3 minutes.
- **Numbers, after:** measured on the two-processor runner that built ticket #249, the 65 files took 2 minutes 32 seconds one after another and 1 minute 42 seconds at the same time, about a third less. Most of the time is the system starting processes, which two processors share. The whole of `pnpm test` then took 2.0 minutes on the two-processor runner of pull request #252's fix round, against the 2.8 before; the `checks` job's own time is on that pull request's Checks tab.
- **What it saves:** worked out from those figures, about 0.9 minutes in each of the two jobs, so each drops a billed minute only when that takes it under a minute boundary; the wait for the reviews shortens either way.
- **Automated:** yes.

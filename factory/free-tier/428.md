### #428. The engineer reviews a build before handing it over

- **Set:** the `factory-engineer` and `implement` skills have the engineer run the `review-architecture` and `review-code` skills on its draft pull request, each as a subagent, once, and fix what would fail a review, before it marks the pull request ready (`decisions.md` [A428a](../decisions/428.md)). Adam and Ryan still review it in `ci` (`decisions.md` [A428b](../decisions/428.md)).
- **Protects:** Actions minutes.
- **Why:** a finding made in `ci` costs a fix round: about 20 billed minutes, 8 for the engineer's job, 1 to start it and 11 for the `ci` run it repeats ([entry #388](388.md)). The same finding made inside the build costs the engineer's fix alone, in a job that already runs.
- **Numbers, before:** from 14 to 18 November 2026, 84 of 171 pull requests had a fix round, so 51% passed with none; there were 164 rounds, a reviewer failed in 100 of the 141 failed `ci` runs, and a ticket took 51 minutes on average ([entry #388](388.md)).
- **Numbers, expected:** about 850 minutes less over the same 171 tickets, worked out and not measured: half of the 120 rounds a review started, 60 rounds at 20 minutes, less 2 minutes more on each of 177 builds ([entry #388](388.md)).
- **What it costs:** Claude usage, on every build: two more reviews, where a pair of reviews in `ci` costs about $2.50 at API prices (`decisions.md` [A266b](../decisions/266.md)). The subscription is the one part that is not free.
- **How it is judged:** `factory/scripts/measures.sh` prints the share of tickets passing with no fix round, to set against 51%, and `factory/scripts/minutes.sh` the minutes a ticket, to set against 51.
- **Automated:** yes. The skills are copied into every project, and no setting is made.

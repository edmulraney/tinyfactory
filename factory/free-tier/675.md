### #675. A review GitHub ran nothing for is looked for in the idle look

- **Set:** `missed-reviews.sh` runs as a step of the existing `flag-idle.yml` job, every six hours, and has no schedule of its own (ticket #675; `decisions.md` [A675a](../decisions/675.md)).
- **Protects:** Actions minutes.
- **Why:** GitHub runs no workflow for a review of a pull request in conflict, so no event is left to react to; a merge comes too seldom to wait for alone.
- **Numbers, as set:** worked out, not measured: a workflow cannot run before it merges. The step makes one call to list the open pull requests and up to four for each one in conflict, a few seconds in a job billed a whole minute, as [entry #271](271.md) counts it: no minute is added while the job stays under one.
- **Numbers, the alternatives:** a job of its own every hour, 24 minutes a day and about 730 a month, 37% of the plan's 2,000, to act within an hour rather than six.
- **Automated:** yes.

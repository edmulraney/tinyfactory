### #160. A review is carried forward over a push that only merges `main`

- **Set:** the `adam` and `ryan` jobs in `ci.yml` first run `carry-review.sh`. When the reviewer's latest verdict is a pass, every commit since the one it passed is a merge of `main`, and the pull request's own change reads the same, the script posts the pass again and the job starts no agent (ticket #160; `decisions.md` [A160a](../decisions/160.md) to [A160d](../decisions/160.md)).
- **Protects:** Claude usage and Actions minutes.
- **Why:** every push ran both reviews again, and most repeats followed a merge of `main`, made by a fix round for a conflict, that changed nothing the reviewers had read.
- **Numbers, before:** on 16 November the two reviews ran 63 times for 33 merged pull requests and used 335 of the day's 1,589 billed Actions minutes (21%): about 5.3 minutes a pair. A pair costs about $2.50 of Claude usage (pull request #116: $2.53, $2.92 and $2.84 for three rounds that were each a merge of `main`).
- **Numbers, after:** worked out, not measured. A job that carries still starts, to check the branch out and compare, so it is billed one minute: a carried pair is 2 minutes and no Claude usage, about 3 minutes and $2.50 less than a pair that reviews.
- **How often it will carry:** less often than every merge of `main`. Run over the 31 merges of `main` on the branches of pull requests #86 to #153, the comparison finds 9 that left the pull request's own change reading the same; after the other 22, #116's three among them, it read differently (in 18 its changed lines, in 4 only the lines beside them), as it does when a conflict is resolved by hand, and those are reviewed again. `decisions.md` and `free-tier.md` were the files most often involved, and each ticket no longer shares them (`decisions.md` [H130a](../decisions/130.md), [H130e](../decisions/130.md)); after the four merges since, on #149 and #153, the change read the same.
  - Rejected, skipping both jobs by a condition, which would bill nothing: the decision needs the branch's history, so a job would still run to make it (`decisions.md` [A160a](../decisions/160.md)).
- **What a carried pass does not skip:** `checks` and `e2e` run on every push as before, and `ship.sh` asks for `fits-main` when `main` has moved ([entry 10](entry-10.md)), so what the merge could break is still tested.
- **Automated:** yes.

### #569. One job for each ticket closed outside a merge

- **Set:** `close-idea.yml` runs one job, `close-idea.sh`, each time an issue is closed by a person or by an agent's own token (ticket #569; `decisions.md` [T569b](../decisions/569.md)). A ticket `ship.sh` closes after a merge starts no job: `ship.sh` runs the script in the job that merged.
- **Protects:** GitHub Actions minutes in a private repo.
- **Numbers:** the job makes at most four calls to GitHub and is billed as 1 minute of the 2,000 a month. It is the event-driven way: nothing looks for closed slices on a schedule.
  - Rejected, a condition on the job that skips a ticket with no parent: whether the event carries the ticket's parent is not known before the workflow is installed, and a condition that never held would close no idea, in silence.
- **Automated:** yes.

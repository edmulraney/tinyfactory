### #385. The owner chooses a public or private repository at setup

- **Set:** `github.sh` creates the repo public when `PURPOSE.md` holds the line the purpose interview writes for that answer, and private otherwise (ticket #385; `decisions.md` [S3](../decisions/setup.md), [S385a](../decisions/385.md)). A repo that exists keeps its visibility ([S385b](../decisions/385.md)).
- **Protects:** Actions minutes.
- **What happened:** this factory, in a private repo, used the month's 2,000 minutes and $30 more in the first 18 days of November 2026.
- **Numbers:** a private repo has 2,000 minutes a month, and a public one has no limit, as ticket #385 gives them on 18 November 2026. On a public repo the per-ticket minutes in the table are counted against nothing, and Neon's branches bind first.
- **What public costs:** anyone can read the code, the tickets, the decision records and the agents' transcripts on the `sessions` branch. That is why the owner is asked and private is the default.
- **`main` on a public repo:** GitHub allows the ruleset there for nothing, so `github.sh` locks `main` ([entry 7](entry-7.md)), and `workflow-app.sh` puts the workflow app on its bypass list so that the installer's push is not refused (`decisions.md` [S385c](../decisions/385.md)). Neither has run against a real public repo.
- **Automated:** yes. The purpose interview asks, and `github.sh` reads the answer.

### #75. End-to-end tests are skipped when no app code changed

- **Set:** the `e2e` job in `ci.yml` runs `needs-e2e.sh` as its first step, and the steps that test carry a condition on its answer: they run when the pull request changes anything under `apps/` or `packages/`, or the root's `package.json`, `pnpm-lock.yaml` or `pnpm-workspace.yaml`, and otherwise the job ends there (ticket #75; `decisions.md` M75a to M75d). Until ticket #250 the `checks` job asked and `e2e` was skipped by a condition, which billed nothing and held the reviews behind `checks` (entry #250).
- **Protects:** Actions minutes.
- **Why:** every pull request waited for its Vercel preview and ran Playwright against it. A pull request that touches only `factory/`, `.claude/` and documents has the app of `main` in its preview, which was tested when it merged.
- **Numbers, before:** on pull request #72, a factory ticket, the job took 60 and 72 seconds in its two passes and was billed 2 minutes each time; the reviews waited for it.
- **Numbers, after:** worked out, not measured. A job that ends at its first step is billed one minute, so each push to such a pull request uses 1 minute where it used 2: a factory ticket with no fix round goes from 9 or 10 minutes to 9. While the job was skipped by a condition it was billed nothing, and such a ticket took 8 (entry #250).
- **How often:** of the 56 pull requests merged up to #164, 42 changed no app code and 14 did.
- **What it does not save:** Vercel still builds a preview, with its database branch, for every pull request: 1 of the 100 deployments a day and one of Neon's 9 preview slots (entry 8). Ticket #165 holds that.
- **When the changed files cannot be read:** the job runs, at the old cost (`decisions.md` M75b).
- **Automated:** yes.

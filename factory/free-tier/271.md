### #271. Idle tickets are looked for four times a day

- **Set:** `flag-idle.yml` runs every six hours, at 00:43, 06:43, 12:43 and 18:43 UTC, as one job of two steps: `rerun-refused.sh`, then `flag-idle.sh`, which flags a ticket in flight left idle for 60 minutes (ticket #271; `decisions.md` [T271a](../decisions/271.md) to [T271g](../decisions/271.md)).
- **Protects:** Actions minutes, and the owner's time.
- **Why:** a schedule is the one thing that notices what did not happen, and every look is billed a whole minute whether or not it finds anything.
- **Numbers, as set:** worked out, not measured: a workflow cannot run before it merges. 4 looks a day at 1 billed minute each, as `review-notes.yml`'s one-step job is billed ([entry #241](241.md)): 4 minutes a day, about 122 of the month's 2,000, or 6%, the minutes of about 13 tickets at 9 each. An idle ticket is flagged between 1 and 7 hours after it was last touched.
- **Numbers, the alternatives:** hourly, 24 minutes a day and about 730 a month, 37% of the plan, to flag within 2 hours. Daily, 1 minute a day and about 30 a month, to flag within 25 hours, with one of three places held meanwhile.
- **What it saves:** nothing in minutes. On 16 November two approvals, a build and four retros refused for billing waited until a person ran each again (`decisions.md` [H220a](../decisions/220.md)); they are now run again within six hours of jobs starting, with nobody asking.
- **Not run again:** a look that was itself refused, so an outage costs no look twice (`decisions.md` [T271g](../decisions/271.md)).
- **Automated:** yes.

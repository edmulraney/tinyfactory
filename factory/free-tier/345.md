### #345. The benchmark starts by hand, in one job

- **Set:** `benchmark.yml` starts on `workflow_dispatch` alone and reviews its cases one after another in one job, with `timeout-minutes: 45` (ticket #345; `decisions.md` A345a to A345d).
- **Protects:** Actions minutes, and the Claude subscription's usage.
- **Numbers:** worked out, not measured in Actions. The test of 17 November 2026 (#344) ran the 12 reviews of the first six pull requests at 70 seconds and $1.21 each with Fable 5.2: 14 minutes and about $14.50 a run. With the checkout and the install that is about 16 billed minutes, under 1% of the month's 2,000. The time limit is about three times that, for a slower model.
- **Why not a job for each case, or two reviews at once:** six jobs would each round up to a whole minute and install Claude Code again. Two reviews at once would save about 7 minutes of a run made when a new model appears, and would draw on the subscription's limit twice as fast.
- **Automated:** yes.

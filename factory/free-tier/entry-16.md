### 16. Three tickets in flight at most

- **Set:** `start-build.sh`, the first step of `implement.yml`, starts a build only when fewer than three other tickets are in flight: issues labelled `in-progress`, and open pull requests the factory opened, those waiting for the owner included (ticket #64; `decisions.md` [T64a](../decisions/64.md) to [T64f](../decisions/64.md)). Before, the rule was one ticket in flight, kept by the `promote` skill alone.
- **Protects:** Neon's 10 branches, Actions minutes and the Claude subscription's usage limits.
- **Numbers, database branches:** each open pull request holds one of the 9 preview slots ([entry 8](entry-8.md)), so three in flight hold 3. That leaves 6 for pull requests merged since checks last ran, a person's own pull requests, and branches a cut-short build left ([entry 13](entry-13.md)). At one in flight it was 1 of 9; with no limit, the ninth open pull request would take the last slot and the next preview would fail.
- **Numbers, Actions minutes for the test with `main`:** [entry #455](455.md). A ticket that merges while nothing else waits is not tested a second time, and pull requests that wait together share one test. A merge that leaves another pull request in conflict costs that one a fix round, about 7 minutes and $0.60 ([entry 14](entry-14.md)); the `promote` skill tells the owner when two tickets are likely to edit the same lines.
- **Numbers, the check itself:** four reads from GitHub and one label change, a few seconds in a job that already runs, so no Actions minutes. A refused ticket ends its run in under a minute, billed as one, and starts no retro (`decisions.md` [T64f](../decisions/64.md)).
  - Rejected, a job of its own holding one concurrency group, to settle two tickets labelled at the same moment: a billed minute on every ticket, 9 minutes becoming 10 (`decisions.md` [T64d](../decisions/64.md)).
- **Claude usage:** not a figure the factory can read. Three builds at once draw on one subscription's limits three times as fast as one; a session the limit cuts short is not continued: its work is kept, and no agent starts on the held model until the limit resets, though the runs that begin meanwhile start on the second model ([entry 13](entry-13.md); [entry #395](395.md); `decisions.md` [A231b](../decisions/231.md), [A663a](../decisions/663.md)).
- **Automated:** yes.

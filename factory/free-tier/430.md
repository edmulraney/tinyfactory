### #430. No retro after a single fix round

- **Set:** after a merge, `ship.sh` starts a retro only for a pull request that had two or more fix rounds, or a round the owner asked for; one fix round starts none (ticket #430; `decisions.md` [A430a](../decisions/430.md), [A430b](../decisions/430.md)). The fix cap and a failed job of the factory's own workflows start their retro as before ([entry 11](entry-11.md)), and an engineer who ends a fix round with no push and asks for a person starts one then.
- **Protects:** Actions minutes, and the Claude subscription's usage.
- **Why:** one fix round is ordinary. Of the 171 pull requests of 14 to 18 November 2026, 84 had one or more ([entry #388](388.md)).
- **Numbers, before:** in those five days 45 pull requests merged after exactly one fix round, and 50 retros ran for them at 3.2 billed minutes each: about 160 of the 473 minutes `retro` was billed, and a session's Claude usage each.
- **Numbers, after:** worked out, not measured: none of those 50 retros starts. `factory/scripts/minutes.sh <from> <to>` over a later week shows it, as fewer `retro` runs than merged tickets with a fix round.
- **What it costs:** a retro's lesson is the record a later retro reads to see a cause repeat, so a cause is seen to repeat only among runs that got a retro (`decisions.md` [A8](../decisions/70.md)).
- **Automated:** yes.

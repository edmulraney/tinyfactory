### #467. A build stopped at its time limit starts again once

- **Set:** `flag-unfinished.sh` starts a build again, from its branch, when the build's job stopped at its time limit, and asks a person to split the ticket when the build before it stopped there too (ticket #467; `decisions.md` [A467a](../decisions/467.md) to [A467c](../decisions/467.md)).
- **Protects:** Actions minutes, and Claude usage.
- **Why:** a build with no bound on its restarts could spend a job's whole time limit again and again on a ticket too large for one session.
- **Numbers, before:** ticket #455 was built twice on 19 November 2026, in runs 44575801012 and 44588133801. Each ran the job's whole 60 minutes, 120 billed minutes together, and each stop waited for the owner to add `ready` again.
- **Numbers, after:** worked out, not measured, because the workflow cannot run before it is installed. A ticket spends at most two time limits in a row, the 120 minutes #455 spent, before a person is asked, and the second build starts with no wait for a person. Each stop still starts one retro ([entry 11](entry-11.md)).
  - Rejected, starting again every time: nothing bounds what one ticket spends.
  - Rejected, asking a person after the first stop: on #455 a second hour could finish what the first began.
- **Event-driven:** the build's own ending starts the next build, through `start-waiting.sh`, when a place is free.
- **Automated:** yes.

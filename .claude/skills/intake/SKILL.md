# Intake

Most insights from one interview become signals to watch, not tickets.

1. **Read** the whole transcript, `PURPOSE.md` and `product/signals.md`.
2. **Write** `product/research/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<who-or-topic>.md`: who was interviewed (role and context; personal details only as the user approves), date and source; what they want to do and what stops them, quoting the important lines; what surprised us or contradicts `PURPOSE.md`.
3. **Sort each insight into one outcome:**
   - **Build now:** a ticket (the `ticket` skill), only when the problem is clear, fits `PURPOSE.md`, and is cheap and obviously right (Tweak, Bug) or already has strong evidence (Initiative). Nothing is built until the user adds `ready`.
   - **Watch:** `product/signals.md`. Add evidence to a matching signal, or add one: title, one line on the problem, evidence list. Merge duplicates. Say when one reaches three independent sources, counting none marked synthetic: it can be proposed as an Initiative.
   - **Drop:** one line in the summary saying why.
4. **Report:** tickets with numbers, signals added or strengthened, what was dropped. Raise separately an insight that challenges `PURPOSE.md`; never edit it.

Never invent evidence or strengthen a quote; record an ambiguous line as ambiguous.

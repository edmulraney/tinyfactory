# Purpose

## Mission

Rapidly validate an idea at zero cost.

## What it is

tinyfactory is a small software factory that one command sets up: AI agents take a technical founder's idea to a validated MVP on free tiers, with a human near the loop instead of in it.

## If it works

A founder with an idea spends their time with customers and on the product, not on writing and reviewing every change. The factory refines the idea into tickets, builds them, reviews them and ships them, and the codebase stays sound as it grows. An idea that gets traction carries on in the same factory, on the same stack.

## Who it is for

A technical founder, or a tiny team, who wants to validate an idea with real users before spending money on it.

## The problem

Agents that build a product by themselves let it decay: each change looks fine, and the codebase gets steadily worse. A founder copes by reading every change, which puts them back in the loop, or by not reading, which ends in slop. Setting up the tickets, checks, reviews, hosting and database that would prevent this takes days nobody spends on an unproven idea.

## How we will know it is working

A founder who did not write tinyfactory runs `npx tinyfactory <name>` and ships a product to real users with it. This repository is built by its own factory, and most of its pull requests merge with no person reading them.

## What it will not do

- It is not an enterprise factory, and it does not serve a large team.
- It does not offer a choice of stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind and shadcn/ui on Vercel and Neon, chosen once.
- It does not leave the free tiers before an idea is proven.
- It does not ask a person to run a command to operate it.
